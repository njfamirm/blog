SONIC CYPHER

AIR-GAPPED AUDIO DATA TRANSFER PROTOCOL.

Transmits on a near-ultrasonic carrier (17–19.6 kHz) that most adults cannot hear. Raise the volume on the sending device and allow microphone access on the receiving one. Switch to the audible band if the two devices are far apart or the speaker is weak.
